# About this Repo

This repo is a collection of write-ups from investigations to home-lab projects.<br>
The goal is to document my knowledge, experience, and growth in the field of cybersecurity.

Write-ups are added/updated periodically and at random when I have the time. My style of writing is to rather than just giving step-by-step instructions, I want to make sure I understand the hows, whys, ins and outs of what I'm doing. Therefore I try to explain the process as I go through it. While this repo is mostly for myself, I do hope that my style of writing may help others in their learning journey, should they find this repo.
